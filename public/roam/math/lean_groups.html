Group Axioms

A group (G,⋅) satisfies associativity, has an identity element, and inverses. In Mathlib this is the Group typeclass.

Lean 4basic
variable {G : Type*} [Group G]

-- Uniqueness of identity
example (e : G) (h : ∀ a, e * a = a) : e = 1 := by
  have := h 1
  simp at this
  exact this

-- Cancellation law
example (a b c : G) (h : a * b = a * c) : b = c := by
  exact mul_left_cancel h

Abelian Groups

In an abelian (commutative) group, ab=ba.

Lean 4abelian
variable {A : Type*} [CommGroup A]

-- In an abelian group: (ab)⁻¹ = a⁻¹b⁻¹
example (a b : A) : (a * b)⁻¹ = a⁻¹ * b⁻¹ := by
  rw [mul_inv_rev, mul_comm]

#check @mul_comm

Subgroups

A subgroup H≤G is a subset closed under multiplication and inverses.

Lean 4subgroups
open Subgroup in
-- The center of a group is always a subgroup
example (G : Type*) [Group G] : Subgroup G := center G
