Chang He (Aaron)

I work on quantum computing and theoretical computer science, with a standing interest in the algebra underneath both.

I am an undergraduate at UNSW Sydney, doing research with Youming Qiao at UTS. Most of my time goes to problems where a question about computation turns out to be a question about structure — group and tensor isomorphism, the limits of quantum speed-up, and what the Fourier picture buys you.
