What inspired the animation

The feel of the flight — a slow pass through a sparse field, seen from something moving through it, with objects trailing ribbons — was inspired by Paper Planes, made by Active Theory for Google in 2016. No code, assets, geometry, shaders or textures from that project are used here. Everything was written from scratch, and the subject matter and interaction are different in the first place. The acknowledgement is here because credit for an idea is owed even when nothing is copied.

Visual note

The motion is informed by standard quantum-circuit notation and by Shor's original algorithm paper; it is an authored visual system, not a live quantum-computing service.

If the flight is not running

Animation stops when the tab is hidden. Reduced-motion keeps the 3D composition as a still frame; missing JavaScript or WebGL leaves this complete document instead. You can always switch views from the top bar, or force the document with ?flat=1.
